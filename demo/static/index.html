Chunk selection under fixed extraction budgets

Which chunks are worth paying an LLM to read?

GraphRAG makes one LLM call per chunk to build its knowledge graph, so indexing cost grows with the corpus. If we can only afford to extract a share B of the chunks, does a clever selector pick better chunks than a random one?

Everything except the selector is held fixed. Any difference in answer quality at a budget B comes from which chunks went to the extractor.

End-to-end: 75 HotpotQA questions

Token F1 of the generated answers, with 95% bootstrap intervals. Random = mean of 3 shuffles.

1. The selectors keep about as much gold evidence as random, within ~8 points at every budget, and never significantly better at 5% or 10%.
2. An oracle keeps all gold evidence at 25%. At budgets up to 25% the selectors close only ~8% of that gap.
3. End to end, F1 rises from 0.07 (10%) to 0.61 (100%). None of 18 strategy-vs-random comparisons is significant after Holm correction.
On pooled multi-hop QA corpora, how much you extract matters far more than which of these selectors picks it.
